03ROOM 01 / COFFEE

WEST AFRICA / LATIN AMERICA

一杯咖啡，
兩種氣候。One cup.
Two climates.

把咖啡帶回產地：西非的象牙海岸，和拉丁美洲的巴西。產量波動不會自己說明原因，但可以提醒我們追問降雨、溫度、品種和耕作。

Trace coffee to Côte d’Ivoire in West Africa and Brazil in Latin America. A change in output does not explain itself—it invites questions about rainfall, heat, varieties and farming.

Official figures show output nearly halved while cultivated area changed less. These numbers alone do not prove that climate was the only cause.

A CIAT study used 21 global climate models to estimate Arabica suitability in the 2050s. A suitability map is not a harvest forecast—and it does not represent Robusta.

04ROOM 02 / RICE + BANANA

SOUTH ASIA / SOUTHEAST ASIA

水退之後，
還能種什麼？After the water,
what can grow?

孟加拉稻田面對鹽水和淹水的挑戰；湄公河三角洲也仰賴季節性的水與土壤。耐受品種能帶來選擇，但不能取代安全水源、農田管理與農民的決策。

Rice in Bangladesh faces salinity and flooding. The Mekong Delta also depends on seasonal water and soil conditions. Resilient varieties create options, but cannot replace safe water, farm support or farmers’ choices.

05ROOM 03 / WILD FISH

NORTH AMERICA / GLOBAL OCEAN

海洋改變，
魚往哪裡去？When oceans shift,
where do fish go?

從阿拉斯加白令海的魚群評估，看到北美案例；再把鏡頭拉遠，理解全球野生捕撈與海洋化學。不同魚種的反應不一樣，野生捕撈也不能和養殖混為一談。

Start with fish-stock assessments in Alaska’s Bering Sea, then zoom out to global wild capture and ocean chemistry. Species respond differently, and wild capture is not aquaculture.

FAO 2026 年發布的 2024 年統計：捕撈水生動物約 9,200 萬噸，其中海洋約 8,000 萬噸；養殖超過 1 億噸。這些是水生動物產量，不等於只計魚類。

FAO’s 2024 figures, released in 2026: about 92 million tonnes of captured aquatic animals, including 80 million tonnes from marine waters; aquaculture exceeded 100 million tonnes. These totals cover aquatic animals, not fish alone.

SOURCE / FAO SOFIA 2026 ↗
ALASKA / EBSNOAA / 2019

不是每種魚，都一樣脆弱Not every fish is equally vulnerable

36個魚群與無脊椎動物群體FISH & INVERTEBRATE STOCKS↗

NOAA 評估白令海 36 個魚群與無脊椎動物群體，指出部分岩魚、平頭鰈與帝王蟹脆弱度較高。結果依物種、棲地與評估方法而異。

A NOAA assessment examined 36 Bering Sea fish and invertebrate stocks. Some rockfish, flathead sole and Tanner crab ranked among the most vulnerable. Results vary by species, habitat and method.

SOURCE / NOAA FISHERIES ↗ STOCK ASSESSMENTS / STOCK SMART ↗
OCEAN CHEMISTRYOBSERVATIONS

酸化，先改變了海水的化學Acidification changes ocean chemistry first

NOAA 的 OCADS 收錄海洋碳與酸化觀測、實驗及模型資料。貝類和部分浮游生物會直接受影響；魚類可能經由食物網承受間接影響，不應描述成魚的外殼正在溶解。

NOAA’s OCADS archives ocean-carbon observations, experiments and models. Shellfish and some plankton can be directly affected; fish may be affected through food webs. Fish do not have dissolving shells.

SOURCE / NOAA OCADS ↗
